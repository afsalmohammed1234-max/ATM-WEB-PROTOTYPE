Design a premium, modern, responsive corporate website for a food-tech company called **ANY TIME MAAVU**.

The website should look like a real, high-end, professional commercial food-tech brand, not a generic template.

Any Time Maavu develops innovative **batter dispensing machines** designed for commercial kitchens and food businesses. The machine helps businesses dispense idli and dosa batter in a more hygienic, consistent, efficient and convenient way.

The brand should feel like a **modern food-technology and commercial kitchen equipment company**, NOT like a restaurant or traditional food brand.

### TARGET AUDIENCE

Design the website primarily for:

* Hotels
* Restaurants
* Catering businesses
* Cloud kitchens
* Messes
* Idli & dosa restaurants
* Commercial kitchens
* Food business owners
* Kitchen managers

The website's main goal is to generate **product enquiries and sales leads**.

---

# WEBSITE STYLE

Create a premium B2B technology aesthetic.

Visual direction:

* Modern
* Clean
* Minimal
* Premium
* Industrial
* Food-tech
* Hygienic
* Trustworthy
* Professional

Use a strong visual hierarchy with generous whitespace.

### COLOR PALETTE

Use the existing Any Time Maavu brand identity as inspiration.

Primary: Deep navy / dark blue
Secondary: Teal / cyan
Background: White and very light grey
Text: Dark navy / charcoal

Use blue and teal primarily for accents, buttons, icons and highlights.

Avoid excessive gradients, flashy colors, cartoon illustrations or generic restaurant aesthetics.

### TYPOGRAPHY

Use a modern, highly readable sans-serif typeface.

Headings should be bold and confident.
Body text should be clean and easy to read.

Use large headlines and short paragraphs.

# IMPORTANT DESIGN RULES

Do NOT make the website look like:

* A restaurant
* A food delivery website
* A generic machinery manufacturer
* An outdated industrial website

Instead make it feel like:

**FOOD TECHNOLOGY × MODERN INDUSTRIAL DESIGN × PREMIUM B2B BRAND**

Use the actual Any Time Maavu logo and product photographs when provided.

Do not generate fake technical specifications.

Do not invent certifications, awards, customers or statistics.

Use image placeholders wherever actual company assets are required.

The final design should look like a website that a serious commercial kitchen equipment company could launch professionally.
